# Sheps.Design website — design rules

- Surface backgrounds (cream #F7F3F1 and any other section surface color) always run full browser width, edge to edge. Content stays inside the 1520px max-width container. Implement with `box-shadow:0 0 0 100vmax <color>;clip-path:inset(0 -100vmax);` on the section alongside its `background`.
- Copy doc: `uploads/sheps-design-site-draft.md` mirrors the live site copy. Whenever on-page text changes (any page, card, caption or subhead), update the matching passage in that doc in the same turn.
- Analytics: every page (any new top-level page DC, and its copy in `site/`) must include `<script src="./analytics.js"></script>` inside its `<helmet>`. This loads Mixpanel (token lives in `analytics.js` — don't duplicate it). Pages in subfolders use the correct relative path (e.g. `../analytics.js`). Shared components (SiteFooter, diagrams) don't need it. Named events use `window.mixpanel && window.mixpanel.track('Event name', { page: location.pathname })`.
- Agent/SEO files: `site/sitemap.xml`, `site/robots.txt`, `site/llms.txt`, and the JSON-LD block in the home page `<head>`. When a page is added, renamed or removed, or services/positioning copy changes, update these in the same turn (and bump sitemap `lastmod`). Drafts (e.g. /insights) stay out of the sitemap and llms.txt.
- Case study videos: every new or replaced `.mp4` in `assets/case-study/` needs two companion files next to it: `<name>-h264.mp4` (H.264, max 1920px wide, CRF 24) and `<name>-mobile.mp4` (H.264, max 1080px wide, 30fps, CRF 26, under ~2 MB). Phones load `-mobile`; desktop browsers without AV1 load `-h264`. When Alex adds a video, remind him to export both versions before it goes live, and copy all three into both `assets/case-study/` and `site/assets/case-study/`.
- Drafts: `/insights` and `/highfive` are drafts. On the live site `vercel.json` redirects them (and their `.html` paths) to `/` with a temporary redirect, they carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`, and they stay out of the sitemap and llms.txt. To publish one, remove its redirects and the noindex meta, add it to sitemap.xml, llms.txt and the home page, and remove its `Disallow` from robots.txt.
