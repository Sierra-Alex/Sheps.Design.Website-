Why agent ready matters.

Most design systems were built for humans to read. That was fine until AI started writing the code. An agentic design system is one where the tokens, components, and rules are structured so an AI can compose from them correctly, and get rejected when it doesn't. That distinction is what helps keep AI tooling on brand and speed up design production.
